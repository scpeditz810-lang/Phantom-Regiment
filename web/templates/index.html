Syndicate History & Origins

Established on September 18, 2026, Phantom Regiment was formed as an autonomous, high-discipline branch of TS Clan. Engineered to lead strategic tactical engagements, tactical arena battles, and unified resource accumulation across multiple allied servers.

With a disciplined hierarchy governed by the Server Owner and vetted Server Managers, Phantom Regiment enforces rigorous operational standards, competitive gaming events, and an advanced decentralized medals economy.
