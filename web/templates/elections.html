ELECTORAL CONSTITUTION // SERVER BYLAWS

Campaign Bylaws & Integrity Rules

1. Filing Fee: 400,000 Medals

Staking medals guarantees only serious leaders run. The fee is non-refundable, but mayors can earn back up to 175k via daily salary.

2. 5 Candidate Limit

To keep campaigns competitive and engaging, each 48-hour election cycle accepts a maximum of 5 registered candidates.

3. Anti-Self-Vote Policy

Candidates are strictly barred from voting for themselves. You must rally real server members and squads to win!

4. 14-Day Term & Roles

The winner automatically receives the hoisted gold 🏛️ Mayor role at the top of the member list.
